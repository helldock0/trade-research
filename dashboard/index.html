How to place the trade (v3)

NAS100, 5-minute chart. IST times are for US summer time; add 1 hour from early November to mid-March.

  1. Mark the range. The highest high and lowest low of the 5-minute candles from 4:30 PM to 7:30 PM IST (7:00–10:00 AM New York).
  2. Wait for a break. From 7:30 PM IST, wait for a 5-minute candle to close above the high or below the low. A wick alone doesn't count.
  3. Wait for the confirming candle. The next candle must also close outside the range on the same side. If it closes back inside, forget that break and keep waiting.
  4. Enter at its close. Buy above the high, sell below the low. Skip the day if this candle closes below the break candle's low minus 2 points (for a buy) or above its high plus 2 points (for a sell).
  5. Set the stop. 2 × ATR(14) on the 5-minute chart, read at entry, away from your entry price. On TradingView set the ATR smoothing to SMA to match the backtest.
  6. Set the target. 4 × the stop distance (4R).
  7. Size the position. Lots = money at risk ÷ (stop distance in points × value of 1 point for 1 lot). Prop account: risk 1% of the starting balance. Your broker's contract details give the point value.
  8. Leave it alone. No moving the stop and no partial closes. The trade can stay open overnight and over the weekend.
  9. One trade at a time, one per day. Only the first confirmed break counts. No new trade while one is open, and no new entries after 2:25 AM IST (3:25 AM in winter).

How the test fills trades

  • Signals use bid candles, as a chart shows them. Longs buy at ask and sell at bid; shorts sell at bid and buy back at ask, so every trade pays the spread: Dukascopy's real one, raised to at least Blueberry's 1.26 points.
  • Swap is booked for each 5 PM New York rollover (2:30 AM IST in summer) a trade is held through: −0.0202% of the entry price a night for longs, +0.0026% for shorts, three nights on Friday. That is Blueberry's −$58.50 / +$7.60 per lot at $10 a point and NAS100 near 29,000, applied to every year.
  • The target is 4× the distance from the actual fill to the stop.
  • If a candle opens past the stop or target (a gap), the exit fills at that open. If one candle reaches both, it counts as a stop.
  • One trade per day: the first confirmed break. A day is skipped if a trade is still open or the entry candle closes beyond the stop.
  • The stop is 2× the 14-candle ATR on 5-min candles, measured at entry. The day is still skipped if the entry candle closes beyond the break candle's low (long) or high (short).
  • v3 replaces v2 (same entry, stop beyond the break candle) and v1 (first close outside the range, no confirming candle).
  • No new entries after 4:55 PM New York (2:25 AM IST in summer). The CFD closes at 4:15 PM New York, so in practice entries run until the close.
